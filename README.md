# The Last Classroom

## About the Series
**Title:** The Last Classroom  
**Genre:** Mystery, Thriller, Drama  
**Seasons:** 2  
**Episodes:** 16  
**Status:** Fictional Series  

## Description
The Last Classroom follows a group of college students who discover an abandoned classroom inside their university. Every midnight, the classroom mysteriously opens and reveals events that have not happened yet.

As they investigate, they uncover dark secrets connected to their school's past. They must work together to solve the mystery before one of them disappears forever.

## Main Characters
- **Alex** - A brave student who leads the group.
- **Sofia** - An intelligent student who loves solving mysteries.
- **Daniel** - A technology expert who investigates clues.
- **Emma** - A curious student who discovers the classroom.
- **Ryan** - A loyal friend who protects the group.

## Story Highlights
1. The students discover the abandoned classroom.
2. Strange messages begin appearing on the blackboard.
3. The group investigates mysterious disappearances.
4. They uncover secrets about their university.
5. The students must change their fate before midnight.

## Why I Like This Series
I like this series because it combines mystery, suspense, and friendship. The story shows the importance of teamwork, courage, and critical thinking when facing difficult situations.
